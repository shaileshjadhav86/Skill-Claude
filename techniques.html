Cinematic Flow

A visual bible of cinematography techniques used in every ad we produce — each with a definition, film grammar, and an AI prompt you can run.

01

Camera Movement

How the camera physically moves through space defines the energy, pace, and emotional weight of a shot. Each movement type sends a distinct psychological signal to the viewer.

02

Framing & Shot Size

The distance between the camera and subject determines intimacy, context, and what information the viewer receives. Shot size is the sentence structure of visual storytelling.

03

Camera Angles

The vertical position of the camera relative to the subject shapes power, perspective, and mood. Angles are one of the fastest ways to communicate status without a single word.

04

Lighting

Light is the most powerful invisible actor in any frame. It sculpts faces, creates depth, sets mood, and signals era — all before a single line is spoken or product is shown.

05

Composition

Where elements are placed within the frame determines where the eye travels and what the viewer feels. Composition is the silent architecture of every great shot.

06

Lenses & Optics

The lens choice changes the physics of the image — compression, distortion, depth of field, and character. Two shots of the same subject look completely different through different glass.
